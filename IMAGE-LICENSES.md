## Review and intended use

- Downloaded from the original Pexels CDN; resized and converted locally to JPEG and WebP. No generative imagery is used.
- Visual inspection: fabric-only photographs; no visible people, logos, artwork or watermarks were found.
- Usage: illustrative textile imagery within a business website, not resale of stock, a trademark, a competing image service, or an endorsement claim.
- Captions and product notices clearly identify the images as illustrative; they do not document actual R.G. Traders inventory, premises or staff.
- Detailed source records, check dates, download URLs and SHA-256 hashes are stored in `licenses/images/*.json` and `data/photos.json`.
- A public credits page links every original photo and photographer. Attribution is provided even though the Pexels licence does not require it.
- Legacy images with unknown provenance were moved out of `public/` to `source-assets/unverified-legacy-images/` and are excluded from the production export. Placeholder SVG slots are also outside the public directory.
- Brand SVG/PNG was drawn in source; Lucide icons use the installed library licence. Google fonts are served from the supplied Google Fonts stylesheet.
